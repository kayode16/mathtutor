Do you teach students outside Nigeria? +

Yes. Online Mathematics tutoring is available to international students, subject to suitable scheduling and curriculum requirements.

Can you help with GCSE and IGCSE Maths? +

Yes. We support GCSE and IGCSE learners with mathematical concepts, examination-style questions and structured revision.

What if my child struggles with the basics? +

We start by identifying difficult topics and recommend a plan that builds understanding before moving on to more advanced material.

Can you prepare my child for WAEC, NECO or JAMB? +

Yes. We offer targeted Mathematics support for these examinations, as well as GCE, Common Entrance and BECE.
