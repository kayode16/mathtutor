YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy notice

MathTutor Nigeria collects information that parents or guardians submit through its enquiry form, including the parent or guardian's name, contact details, country, the student's age range, curriculum and the kind of Mathematics support requested.

YouTube videos and Google links

If you choose to play an embedded YouTube lesson, your browser connects to YouTube (using the privacy-enhanced embed domain). YouTube may process device or usage data under its own privacy terms. External Google Business Profile links also take you to Google, where Google's privacy practices apply. Our YouTube players load only when you select Play.

Student feedback screenshots

Where genuine WhatsApp feedback is displayed, we aim to seek permission and remove names, contact numbers, personal photographs and identifying information about children before publication. Feedback screenshots should not be uploaded or published without appropriate consent.

Why we collect this information

We use it to respond to tutoring enquiries, discuss learning needs, recommend appropriate support and arrange lessons where requested.

Form processing

The contact form in this website package is configured for Netlify Forms. Once deployed and activated on Netlify, submitted form data may be processed by Netlify and accessible to the site owner. The downloaded/local prototype does not itself store form submissions.

Children's information

Enquiries should be submitted by a parent or guardian. Please share only information necessary to discuss Mathematics support and avoid sending medical information, financial details, passwords or other sensitive personal information about a child.

Cookies and analytics

The website can use Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel for aggregate visit measurement and contact or assessment-conversion tracking. Optional tracking scripts load only if you choose Accept optional cookies in the website banner. You can reject them without losing access to tutoring information. Earlier choices made on the EMIVANT website are respected on this domain. To change your choice, open cookie settings. The services may process browser and usage information subject to their own policies. Review actual service configuration and applicable requirements before publication.

Requests and contact

To ask about the information submitted or request its deletion, contact segunoladele@gmail.com. A complete production privacy policy should additionally document relevant retention periods, processing arrangements and applicable rights.
